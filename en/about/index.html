About us

Practical sourcing expertise. Long-term partnerships.

We help European industrial businesses build dependable international manufacturing supply chains.

Production support built around your requirements.

We combine manufacturing knowledge, supplier management and commercially independent advice. Our aim is not simply to find a factory, but to establish a robust production solution.

Every enquiry begins with a technical review. We consider material, tolerances, geometry, annual demand, inspection needs and delivery expectations before identifying the right manufacturing route.

We coordinate communication with manufacturing partners and support the project through sampling, production, quality control and delivery. This structured approach reduces sourcing risk and provides greater visibility at each stage.
